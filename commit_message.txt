feat(queue): add clean() and obliterate() for queue retention

Port cleanJobsInSet-3.lua and obliterate-2.lua from BullMQ v5.8 with
their include chain (removeJob, removeParentDependencyKey, cleanList,
cleanSet, batches, ...), flattened to this repo's include convention.
Parent dependency cleanup is wired to the existing flow includes; the
dedup (deid/de:) and scheduler (rjk/repeat) paths are kept as inert
no-ops until those features land.

- Queue::clean(grace, limit, state): removes jobs older than the grace
  period from a state set, chunked 10k per call like Node.
- Queue::obliterate(force): pauses the queue then destroys all of its
  data iteratively; errors on active jobs unless forced.
